A private tool for Oregon state employees

Accommodation Request Builder

Answer a few questions and get a complete, well-structured request to paste into an email or document for your manager — with everything your manager needs to forward to HR / Employee Services on the first pass.

No data stored or sent Runs entirely in your browser Unofficial · not legal advice

Grounded in Oregon policy and law

  • DAS Statewide Policy 50.020.10 — ADA and Reasonable Accommodation in Employment (effective 3/21/2025)
  • DAS Statewide Policy 60.000.30 — Expression of Milk and Unpaid Rest Periods (effective 11/01/2024)
  • ORS 659A.112 (disability) · 659A.146–.147 (pregnancy) · 659A.033 (religious) · 653.077 (expressing milk)
  • OAR 839-006-0206 — Reasonable Accommodation (BOLI administrative rule)
  • BOLI: Pregnancy accommodations at work · Breaks to express breast milk
  • DAS sample Reasonable Accommodation Request form — the field set this tool mirrors

Unofficial community tool. Not affiliated with the State of Oregon, not legal advice, and not a substitute for your agency’s own procedures — those always govern. Completing a form is not required to request an accommodation; verbal requests are equally valid under Statewide Policy 50.020.10. If you need legal advice, consult an attorney. BOLI also answers questions and takes complaints: 971-245-3844.
